With 2 pages on, Scroll mode lays pages out in rows and wide spreads fill a full row. Your view choices (reading mode, fit, zoom, two-page, direction, theme, previews and these settings) are saved automatically and used every time you open a book.

hHide or show the toolbars
gJump to a page number
Right-clickIn the library: mark a comic as read or unread, regenerate its cover (long-press on touch screens)

In Page mode, click the left or right edge to turn the page and the middle to hide the toolbars. Drag a zoomed page to move around it. Wide pages are always shown on their own.
