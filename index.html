Controls

← →Previous / next page (flipped in Manga mode)
Space Shift+SpaceScroll down / up, turning the page at the edge
↑ ↓Scroll a little
Home EndFirst / last page
+ − 0Zoom in / out / reset
Ctrl + wheelZoom toward the cursor (also trackpad pinch)
1 2 3Fit width / fit page / actual size
dTwo-page spreads on or off
vSwitch between Page and Scroll modes
tShow or hide page previews
rRight-to-left (manga) mode
hHide or show the toolbars
gJump to a page number
fFullscreen

In Page mode, click the left or right edge to turn the page and the middle to hide the toolbars. Drag a zoomed page to move around it. Wide pages are always shown on their own.
